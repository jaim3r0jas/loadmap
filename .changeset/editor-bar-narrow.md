An editor in a narrow column (not `fill`, below 560 px of its own width) keeps its own controls on one row: Hide code, Format, New and Export fold into one ⋯ menu, under the headings View, Templates and Export; a page's `start` and `actions` stay in the bar. When the column hides the control the keyboard is on, the keyboard moves to the bar's next stop, and a menu whose button goes closes.
