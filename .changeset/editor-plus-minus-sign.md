While ligatures are on, the editor draws a capacity's `+-` (and `+/-`) as `±`, in the room of its marks, the way a
font joins `->`: the text keeps its marks (copy, undo, the file and a share link are as written) and the cursor steps
over the sign as over one character. A `+-` in a text, or in a file stamped before 1.6 (where it is a plus and a
minus), is drawn as written, and so is every `+-` with ligatures off.
