The diagram's tag says where the limit is, not its rank: "LIMIT · 18×", the multiple at which that component reaches the target (the pessimistic edge of a ± range, the headline's number), and the next two in line wear quiet badges ("2nd · 20×", "3rd · 35×") when they reach it within the load's range. When the limit passes to another component, its old ring fades (its tag already the old box's badge) and the new one comes in, on the engine's clock: never two tags, never two rings at once. A story step that does it adds a shot to the new limit (1.8 s, counted in `playStory()`'s length) and its title says so: "API gateway fixed: the worker pool is next, at 10×", or "The push broker overtakes the position consumer: the limit is 18× now". A component drawn as several boxes is tagged once, and a box that leaves wears nothing. The headline, the scenarios table and the poster say "limited by"; the box detail says "limit", "2nd" or "3rd" as its box does, and the limits list tags its first row "limit". Reduced motion cuts the handover.
