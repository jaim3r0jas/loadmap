The filling editor on a phone (below 720 px wide, or 500 px high): the text or the diagram, switched by Diagram and Code at the bottom in thumb reach; the diagram a canvas a finger pans and two pinch, fitted above a sheet of panels that rests at peek (the verdict and the load slider), half or full, dragged, tapped or stepped with the arrow keys, the inspector in it while the text is hidden; ⋯ opens every other action in a sheet from the bottom; typing, the text is 16 px, the switch steps aside and a row of keys sits on top of the keyboard (Format, Undo, an indent, braces, quotes, Done); the bars clear the notch and the home bar, and every target is 44 px. The caret is drawn in the theme's text colour, light on a dark theme, where it was black. `LoadRange`, the load slider alone, is exported from `@loadmap/react`.
