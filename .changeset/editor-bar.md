A new top bar for the editor: one row of one height at every width, the model's title at its start, then **New** (the templates, now a menu with a line for each), **Export** (the `.loadmap` file) and **Share**, a split button whose menu has **Embed in a page…**. Its tools are one stop for Tab, with the arrow keys between them; each menu opens from the keyboard and Escape closes it. `newItems` and `exportItems` add a page's sections to the two menus. On a phone, the sheet behind ⋯ groups the same actions under headings, the templates on a page of their own. The template list, the phone's sheet and the inspector now load when first needed.
