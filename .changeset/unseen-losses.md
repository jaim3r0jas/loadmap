A loss the definition plans and no drain counts is a warning: what a limiter turns away ("what limiter turns away
vanishes: give it a drain that counts it"), what a flow's `drop` rule drops, what its `timeout` fails. The quick fix
"Add a drain for what limiter loses" adds `node limiterRejected "Turned away" { drain { reason: [rejected] } }` after
the box and the edge into it. A pass, cache or split past capacity warns nothing. The examples, the templates and the
docs' definitions have their drains.
