# Examples

Models to look at and to start from. The walkthroughs are whole systems with a story, each on a page of its own; the
starters are one idea each, a few boxes to open in the editor and make your own. Each card lists what its model
shows, and each walkthrough names the starters it grew from.

## Starters

Ten starters for common shapes, one for each building block among them (a cache, an autoscaled pool, a rate
limiter, retries). Each compiles without a warning, keeps its own expectations, and lays out without a label over
another, in both directions.

**Try:** pick a card, then the scenario beside **Today** to see what the change buys · click where it runs out, under
the slider.

<div class="island" data-island="gallery" data-show="starters" data-root="../"></div>

In your own page, `TEMPLATES` from `@loadmap/editor/templates` has their text, and `<LoadmapEditor>` offers them in its
**New** menu.
