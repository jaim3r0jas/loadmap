# Templates

Six starters for common shapes. Each compiles without a warning, keeps its own expectations, and lays out without a
label over another, in both directions. Pick one to see it; open it in the editor to make it yours.

<div class="island" data-island="templates" data-editor="../editor/"></div>

In your own page, `TEMPLATES` from `@loadmap/editor/templates` has their text, and `<LoadmapEditor>` offers them under
**New from template**.
