## The editor

A `.loadmap` definition beside its live diagram. Errors show under the line as you type, and the diagram keeps the
last version that compiled. Your text is kept in this browser as a draft.

A click on a box selects its lines in the text, and the cursor in a box's lines rings the box. **Format**
(Shift+Alt+F) lays the text out the one way, as `loadmap fmt` does.

**Share link** puts the whole definition and the values you set into a link. It opens with **Hide code**: the diagram
with an inspector beside it. Click a box to change its label, its capacity and the parameters it reads, or open
**Model** for the title, the load, growth and the flows. Each change is a small edit of the text, so **Undo** takes it
back, the link carries it, and **Show code** shows what changed.
